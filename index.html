Services

Four lines of business under one company.

Software, websites, products, and supplies. Solstice Group handles all four directly, with a single point of contact for every inquiry.

  • Apps and software

    We design and build custom mobile and web applications for businesses, including AI-enabled tools. After launch, we maintain them with updates, fixes, and changes as platforms evolve.

    Learn more about apps and software

  • Websites and digital presence

    We design and build websites for local and regional businesses, and set up the hosting behind them. After launch, ongoing maintenance covers content edits and security updates.

    Learn more about websites and digital presence

  • Product distribution

    We source products from manufacturers and wholesale suppliers and sell them through online marketplaces and direct channels. We manage the listings, pricing, and inventory for each channel.

    Learn more about product distribution

  • Business supplies

    We supply janitorial, office, breakroom, and maintenance products to commercial buyers. Buyers open an account with us and place repeat orders against it.

    Learn more about business supplies

How we work

Three steps for every project and order.

  1. Scope

    We confirm what you need, the timeline, and the price in writing before any work or order begins.

  2. Deliver

    We build the software or fill the order, and report progress at the checkpoints we agreed on.

  3. Support

    After delivery, we fix issues, maintain what we built, and handle reorders on your account.
