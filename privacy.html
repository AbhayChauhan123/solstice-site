Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Who we are

This policy covers the website at solsticegroupusa.com, operated by Solstice Group LLC, a Virginia limited liability company (“Solstice Group”, “we”, or “us”). It explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to send us through the contact form or by email:

  • Your name, company or organization, and email address.
  • Your phone number, if you provide it.
  • The inquiry type you select and the message you write.

This website does not use cookies, analytics tools, advertising pixels, or other tracking technologies. The fonts and scripts on this site are served from our own domain, not from third-party networks.

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested. This information is used by the host to deliver and secure the website. We do not use it to identify you.

How we use your information

We use the information you send us to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about it.
  • Prepare quotes, proposals, or orders you request.
  • Keep a record of our business correspondence.
  • Meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not use your information for automated decision making, and we do not add you to a marketing list without your permission.

How your information is shared

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

We use a small number of service providers to operate this website and our business: a form processing service that delivers contact form submissions to our inbox, our email provider, and our website host. These providers handle your information only to perform their service for us and under their own privacy and security terms.

We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Solstice Group or others.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry records for as long as needed to respond and to maintain our business records, and then delete them. If you become a customer, we keep records of our work together for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Security

This website is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. We limit access to inquiry records to Solstice Group and the service providers named above. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email info@solsticegroupusa.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

If this website links to other sites, their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Solstice Group LLC at info@solsticegroupusa.com.
